typing ">" at the start of a new line colors your text green:
>posting on moshr.net

typing "I'm gay" or "im gay" (case insensitive) at the start of a new line colors your text with the gay flag's colors:
I'm gay and I LOVE moshr.net

typing "I'm trans" or "im trans" (case insensitive) at the start of a new line colors your text with the trans flag's colors:
I'm trans and I LOVE moshr.net

typing "THE VOICES" (case insensitive) at the start of a new line gives you a glitch effect:
THE VOICES ARE TELLING ME TO BRUSH MY TEETH!

typing "YAY" (case insensitive) at the start of a new line gives you a vertical shake effect:
YAY THEY SELL VITNER'S CRUNCHY NUGGETS HERE!

typing "POLICE" at the start of a new line colors your text with police siren colors:
POLICE, HELP!

typing "FAHCK" or "WTF" at the start of a new line colors your text red:
FAHCK I TECHED THAT THROW!

typing "faygo" (case insensitive) anywhere in your comment gives you:
MoM wHy DiDn'T yOu GeT tHe PiNeApPlE fAyGo?

typing "!!!", "!!!!", or "!!!!!" at the end of a line gives you a shake effect of various strengths:
I LOVE moshr.net!!!

I LOVE moshr.net!!!!

I LOVE moshr.net!!!!!

typing "?!?", "?!?!", or "?!?!?" at the end of a line gives you a shake effect of various strengths:
You're gay and you love moshr.net?!?

You're gay and you love moshr.net?!?!

You're gay and you love moshr.net?!?!?

typing "..!", "...!", or "....!" at the end of a line gives you a shake effect of various strengths:
You're gay and you love moshr.net..!

You're gay and you love moshr.net...!

You're gay and you love moshr.net....!

typing "..?", "...?", or "....?" at the end of a line gives you an italic effect of various strengths:
You're gay and you love moshr.net..?

You're gay and you love moshr.net...?

You're gay and you love moshr.net....?

typing "\m/" gives you:
\m/

adding "{{{ x }}}" around a selection of text gives you glowing text:
{{{ive been feeling weird ever since i ate that sourdough}}}

adding "[[ x ]]" around a selection of text gives you spoiler text:
[[ I'm gay and I LOVE moshr.net ]]
