what is moshr?
moshr is an ephemeral real-time anonymous text-only forum. you do not need an account to post here.
how do i post?
to make a new thread simply click on the "create new thread" button in the top right corner.
to post in a thread simply click the "create new post" button at the top of the thread or click reply on someones post.
what is a moshpit?
a moshpit is a sub-forum dedicated to a specific topic
how do i make a moshpit?
you dont, moshpits are based on user activity. all you need is one thread for a moshpit to become active
a thread gets scheduled for deletion after 7 days of existence
what is a quirk?
a typing quirk. basically something that modifies text to make it more exciting
how can i support the site?
by being active, by sharing the site with friends, and by donating.
